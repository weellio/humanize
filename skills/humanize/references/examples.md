# Before / After

## Soulless clean to voice (news item)

Before:
> The experiment produced interesting results. The agents generated 3 million lines of code. Some developers were impressed while others were skeptical.

After:
> 3 million lines of code, written while the humans slept. I keep going back and forth on it. Half the dev world is losing their minds, the other half is explaining why it doesn't count. Both can't be right.

Adds: a real reaction, a concrete image, a side.

## LinkedIn: AI-clean to engagement

Before:
> Anthropic's latest model delivered significant improvements in coding and reasoning benchmarks, shifting the AI race toward specialized capabilities. This is an important development for professionals relying on LLMs.

After:
> The AI race just stopped being about size.
>
> A new model jumped 40% on coding and 30% on reasoning without getting bigger.
>
> What surprised me: everyone else is fighting over context windows, and the winner bet on reasoning depth instead.
>
> If you build with LLMs, "just use a bigger model" is quietly becoming the wrong instinct.
>
> Are we near the end of scale-at-all-costs, or will scale absorb this too?

What changed: a hook that stands alone, real numbers, white space, a first-person read, and a question a stranger would answer.

## Article opener: generic to specific

Before:
> In today's rapidly evolving landscape, AI detection tools have become an increasingly important topic for many organizations.

After:
> An AI detector built by writers just flagged every human sample it was given as machine-written. The tool failed the one job it exists for.
