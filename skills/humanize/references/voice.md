# Voice: calibrate, then add

Removal cleans the writing. Voice makes it someone's. This is the half most AI editing skips, and the half that earns engagement.

## Calibrate from a sample

When the user gives you their writing, read it for four things and mirror them:

1. **Sentence length.** Short and punchy, long and flowing, or mixed. Match the ratio, not just the average.
2. **Word-choice level.** Casual, academic, or in between. A plumber and a professor pick different words for the same idea.
3. **Punctuation habits.** Dashes, parenthetical asides, semicolons, one-line paragraphs. These are fingerprints.
4. **Verbal tics.** Recurring phrases, the way they open, the way they transition, the way they end.

**The rule:** do not just delete AI patterns. Replace them with the sample's patterns. Removal leaves a hole; the sample tells you what shape fills it. If they write short, you write short.

No sample? Default to a first-person practitioner voice: a smart person who does the work, talking to peers. Not a brand, not a press desk.

## Add substance: the three moves

### Specificity

Vague: "The study found a range of accuracy across detectors."
Specific: "One detector caught every human sample. Another flagged all of them as AI."

Name who, when, where, how many. Swap "industry observers note" for the actual person and date. A number is a magnet; a category is wallpaper.

### Restraint

Inflated: "This marks a pivotal moment in the evolution of AI detection."
Actual size: "The tool failed the one job it was built for."

Cut "testament," "pivotal," "game-changer," "revolutionary," "landscape." Say what happened. Let the reader decide how big it is. Overselling reads as AI and as marketing at the same time.

### Voice

Wire-copy: "The results were mixed and drew varied reactions."
Voice: "I keep going back and forth on this one. Half the field is thrilled, half says it doesn't count. I lean toward: it counts, and that's the uncomfortable part."

React, do not just report. Use "I" when it is honest. Name the feeling precisely instead of labeling it ("something is off about" beats "this is concerning"). Take a side. Neutral is the sound of no one.

## The soulless test

If the mechanics are clean but the piece still feels like nothing, it has no voice. Ask one question: what does the writer actually think about this? Put the answer in. That single move is usually the difference between a post people scroll past and one they argue with in the comments.
